## Sources and assumptions

- IKEA DVALA: official IKEA German product information for the 140 × 200 cm DVALA line states cotton construction, maximum 60 °C colored wash, and normal-temperature tumble drying.
- Ravensberger: the recorded purchase is Struktura-MED GEL, 140 × 200 cm, H3. The manufacturer specifies the cover material, gentle 60 °C wash, and no tumble drying; its care guide recommends washing a mattress cover around once yearly under normal hygiene conditions.
- ALLCON: the brand's guidance says encasings are intermediate covers and recommends no more than two 60 °C washes per year. The initial 90–95 °C wash and any drying permission must be confirmed against the supplied product label because Allcon qualities differ.
- Amazon ASIN B00Q4TJET4: the linked selected variant is the black Amazon Basics four-piece set with two 140 × 70 cm bath towels and two 50 × 100 cm hand towels. Amazon states 100% cotton, 60 °C machine wash and low-temperature tumble drying.
- Amazon ASIN B0B943PD7Y: the Fit-Flip product page states 88% polyester / 12% polyamide, machine washing up to 60 °C and similar-color washing for the first cycles. The shared parent-product link displays marine blue but does not preserve a selected size, so the purchased size remains unconfirmed.
- The two additional turquoise towels carry matching labels for 100% organic cotton and the same 60 °C wash, low-temperature dryer and low-temperature ironing limits. Their GOTS and OEKO-TEX marks describe certification, not additional wash settings.
- Tee Jays TJ520: the private order email confirms four black shirts in size L. The public Foxxshirts product page confirms the TJ520 construction, 100% organic cotton, 220 g/m² Interlock fabric, pre-shrinking, and a 60 °C maximum wash; it permits ironing but does not state dryer permission.
- The s.Oliver shirt label photo confirms the care-symbol limits but cuts off the fiber-composition line. No matching purchase email or reliable public product page was found from the visible label number, so material and exact model remain explicitly unknown.
- Four Gymshark order confirmations from 2022 and 2026 establish the twelve recorded model, color, size and quantity combinations. Shipping notices were ignored as duplicates. Public Gymshark model pages supply the material compositions recorded above.
- Gymshark's clothing-care guidance recommends washing sportswear inside out, cold or at no more than 30 °C, using mild fragrance-free detergent, avoiding the tumble dryer, and separating colors. These conservative instructions govern the Gymshark load family when an archived model page lists material but no complete symbol set.
- Where a manufacturer gives a cycle but no spin speed, this matrix records the matching program behavior rather than inventing an exact rpm. Reduced/medium spin is a conservative planning assumption for microfiber, encasings and the mattress cover.
