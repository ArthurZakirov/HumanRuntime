# Public/private boundary

HumanRuntime is public. Personal material belongs here only when Arthur has explicitly approved that category for publication.

## Approved public categories

- nutrition routines and Cronometer food mappings
- physical desk, furniture, devices, ergonomics, and charging setup
- household laundry inventory, care labels, machine assumptions, and planning rules
- reusable methods and installation documentation

## Always exclude

- home or delivery addresses and precise residential location
- passwords, tokens, cookies, account identifiers, and authentication details
- health-insurance or other financial account information
- career targets, applications, negotiations, compensation, and private professional strategy
- customer or employer-internal information
- unrelated private notes or records

Before publication, review every changed file for excluded data and credentials. An approved category does not authorize importing unrelated material from a private repository.
